05-focus
  Before click, Invoice Process had settled one fit pass:
    --fit-scale 0.8245, root font 35.4535px
    Company NO box 361.52 x 41.77
  After clicking Company NO:
    --fit-scale 0.7966, root font 34.2538px
    Company NO box 349.41 x 40.39
    Property Street 999.5 x 41.77 became 966 x 40.39
  Cause: one fit pass does not settle, because ch widths and borders do not
  shrink in proportion to the first scale. The follow-up pass was dropped, and
  the next render (focus setState) applied the leftover shrink. Focus CSS does
  not change font-size.
  The audit in e2e/tests/focus-layout.spec.ts compares computed font-size
  (toBeCloseTo precision 1) and parent-relative boxes (1px) for every input,
  button, and dropdown it reaches. 35.4535 vs 34.2538 fails that check.
  After the fix the gate opens already settled at 34.2538px / 0.7966, and
  clicking Company NO or Property Street does not change the font or the boxes.
  See before_focus_font_measurements.json and after_focus_font_measurements.json.
